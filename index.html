BEFORE YOU ASK

Good to know.

Still curious? Just ask us ↗

How do I check a product’s price or availability?+

Use the upgrade request form or message us on WhatsApp with the model, storage, colour and any preferences. Our team will confirm current availability and pricing before you decide.

Can I get a quote before a repair?+

Yes. Tell us your device model and symptoms. Some issues need an in-store diagnosis before we can confirm a quote. We’ll explain the proposed work and cost before proceeding.

Do you deliver?+

Delivery is available in selected areas. Share your location with the store on WhatsApp to confirm coverage, timing and any applicable charge.

Can I pay with Tabby or Tamara?+

Ask the team to check eligibility for your purchase. Payment plans depend on provider approval, limits and current terms. The provider makes the approval decision.

Does submitting a request confirm a booking?+

No. Your request starts a conversation with the store. A repair booking, stock reservation or delivery is confirmed only when our team agrees the details with you.
